tracking metrics is capitalism, right?

It's inherently destructive because it creates a focal point around something which can be optimized, and subjugates the rich, complex experience of all things around it. For a tech startup -- our modern bastions of hyper-capitalism -- that's a good thing. We don't want employees constantly considering the vast richness of their duties; we want them to make their number go up, so that other numbers can go up, so that eventually the number that matters most -- revenue -- goes up.
